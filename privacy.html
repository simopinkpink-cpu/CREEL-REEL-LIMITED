Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026

CREEL & REEL LIMITED respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared and protected when you use our mobile applications, websites and related digital services.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the application or service, we may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system version, application usage information, diagnostics, crash reports, analytics information and advertising identifiers where applicable.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and maintain our applications, improve functionality and performance, understand usage trends, resolve technical problems, prevent abuse and improve the overall user experience.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our websites or third-party services used by our applications may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, device identifiers and similar technologies for functionality, analytics, security or advertising. Where required by applicable law, consent will be requested before non-essential tracking technologies are used.

Location Information

If an application requires location-based functionality, location information may be processed to provide requested features, improve services or support analytics. Location permissions can generally be managed through your device settings.

Third-Party Services

Our applications may use third-party providers for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, hosting, authentication or other services. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

Advertising

Certain applications may display advertisements. Advertising providers may use device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, measure, personalize or limit advertising, subject to applicable law and available user choices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our services, comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

International Data Transfers

Information may be processed or stored in countries outside your country of residence. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also manage certain application permissions and advertising choices through your device or application settings.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to operate our services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or satisfy legal obligations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date where appropriate.

Contact Us

For privacy or support questions, please use the developer contact details listed on the relevant Google Play app page.
